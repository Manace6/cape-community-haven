Welcome to Cape Community Haven

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to providing shelter, meals, and skill-development workshops to vulnerable individuals across the Western Cape.

Become a Volunteer Today

Our Impact

  • Safe Shelter: Supporting hundreds of individuals in need of emergency housing.
  • Nutritious Meals: Serving daily hot meals to local community members.
  • Skills Training: Offering workshops in literacy, basic computer skills, and trade crafts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I support Cape Community Haven?

You can get involved by signing up as a volunteer, donating supplies, or making financial contributions.

Where are your facilities located?

Our main center is based in Cape Town, serving the surrounding Western Cape communities.
