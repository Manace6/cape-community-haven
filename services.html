Our Community Programs

We offer target services designed to rebuild lives and strengthen local communities.

1. Emergency Shelter & Nutrition

Providing safe overnight accommodations, sanitation facilities, and three warm meals daily to individuals in transition.

2. Skills & Vocational Workshops

Equipping adults with practical skill sets, including introductory web literacy, carpentry, and tailoring to assist with job placement.

3. Youth Support & After-School Care

Offering homework assistance, sports activities, and nutritional support for primary and high school students.
