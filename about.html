Our History

Established in 2021, Cape Community Haven started as a small soup kitchen in Cape Town and has grown into a comprehensive community support center providing food, shelter, and educational programs.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower underprivileged communities through sustainable support, shelter, and educational development programs.

Vision: A compassionate society where every individual has access to food, safe shelter, and opportunities for growth.
